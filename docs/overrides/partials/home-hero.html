Trust boundary

No remote export by default. Sensitive or raw context stays withheld by policy, but the withholding stays visible.

Safe to share

Compact metadata, latest blocker, do-not-repeat guidance, and one next action.

What stays out

Raw logs, secrets, full transcripts, and private notes do not get promoted into the default handoff.
